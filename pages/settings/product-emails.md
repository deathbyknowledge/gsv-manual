# Product Emails And Essential Notices

[Back to settings](index.md)

Ship product-update emails are optional. The marketing checkbox on signup starts
unchecked and is separate from agreement to the Terms. You can sign up, sign in,
recover your account and accept a People invitation without opting in.

Open **Settings → Preferences → Product email preferences**. This opens the
Accounts page, where you sign in with your verified owner email and choose
whether to receive product updates. The preference is stored in Accounts and
also applies to subscriptions submitted through the website footer. It is not a
local Ship configuration key or a preference stored only on this device.

A website subscription asks you to confirm by email. An explicit opt-in during
account signup can activate after your email verification, without a second
confirmation. Verifying your address alone never opts you in. Existing accounts
are not automatically subscribed.

You can withdraw in Accounts or with the unsubscribe link in a product email.
A pending status means delivery/confirmation has not completed; it does not
mean you are subscribed. Suppressed addresses are not automatically resubscribed
when you sign up again. Changing your account email does not transfer consent
to the new address.

The Accounts preference page and footer form work without JavaScript. Creating
a Ship still requires JavaScript. If the preference cannot be saved during
signup, you can continue and set it later.

Essential account, security and service notices are separate. They go to
verified owners of affected accounts and do not depend on marketing consent.
They contain no product promotions.
